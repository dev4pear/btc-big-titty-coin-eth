What exactly am I holding?

You hold $BTC (“Big Titty Coin”), a community meme token on Ethereum. It is a separate token from Bitcoin. Always verify the full contract address before trading. The Ethereum CA will be published here when live — do not trust unofficial addresses.

How does holding turn into Bitcoin?

The $BTC market is paired with BTC. Part of the trading fees is distributed to eligible holders as BTC rewards. You keep your $BTC position while distributions arrive in your Ethereum wallet. Rewards depend on trading activity and the project’s distribution rules; amounts and timing are variable.

Is the reward really BTC?

Rewards are Wrapped BTC (WBTC) on Ethereum — a tokenized representation of Bitcoin with 8 decimal places. They are not native Bitcoin-network transfers. Your wallet may label it WBTC.

Where do I buy $BTC?

Do I need to connect my wallet here?

No. This site does not ask for wallet connection, signatures, deposits, or claims. Buy via Uniswap. Hold in your own wallet.
